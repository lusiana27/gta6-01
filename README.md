**Solara City** — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch in plain canvas JavaScript. No Rockstar IP anywhere: the city, the crew, the story and the art are all our own.

Milo Reyes is back in Solara City with nothing but a stolen jacket and a fast right foot. The Sunset Crew has five jobs for him: steal wheels, run a pickup at the East Docks, lose the SCPD heat, rob Solara Bank, and take the gold convertible from the Marina in one last score.

What is in the game:

- A sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car — roads, sidewalks, palms, a beach and the docks
- Traffic that drives the grid and pedestrians who stroll (and flee) the sidewalks
- Cars to steal and drive, with arcade handling, crashes and wrecks
- A wanted level (0–3 stars) with SCPD cruisers that chase, ram and move in for the bust
- Five story missions with objectives, cash rewards and a finale
- Combat on foot: punch and a pistol, with ammo and health pickups
- HUD with cash, health, wanted stars, speed and a live minimap
- Start screen, pause, busted / complete screens, and saves after every finished job (localStorage)
- Synthetic WebAudio sound: engine hum, sirens, gunshots, pickups and mission jingles

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Play with keyboard and mouse: WASD to walk and drive, E to get in and out of a car, F to punch, mouse to aim and shoot, P to pause, M to mute.
